1. Irrigation Workflow

  1. Data Collection: Read data using sensors.
  2. Advisory: Determine if irrigation is necessary or not required.
  3. Water Pump Control: Automatically toggle the water pump system on or off.
  4. Log Report: Store daily irrigation logs.

2. Sensors Used

  • Soil Moisture Sensor: Measures water content in the soil root zone.
  • Temperature Sensor: Tracks temperature so that the plants get the required heat.
  • Humidity Sensor: Measures humidity levels.
  • Rainfall Sensor: Detects or Predicts active precipitation.

3. Crop Stages

Germination
Initial sprouting stage. Requires consistent care.
Tillering
Formation of shoots. Requires moderate water.
Grand Growth
Phase of rapid growth. Requires high water intake.
Maturity
Final development and ripening.
